about

Who's writing this code

I'm Jordan Vale, a backend and systems engineer who cares more about what happens when a service is under real load than how it looks in a demo. Most of my work lives in the layer nobody sees — queues, schemas, retries, the stuff that decides whether a product feels reliable or flaky.

I started out fixing slow SQL queries as a side project and ended up specializing in distributed systems and developer tooling. I like small, well-tested services more than sprawling monoliths, and I write documentation the way I'd want to receive it: short, honest, and up to date.

Outside of production code, I maintain a couple of open-source CLI tools and occasionally write about debugging war stories.
