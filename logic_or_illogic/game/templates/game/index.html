How to play

  1. Read the question. It could be a riddle, a sum, a paradox or pure nonsense. You won't know which.
  2. Write your own answer. No options to pick from. Use your own words.
  3. Get judged fairly. Logical questions are checked for real correctness. Silly or deep ones reward creativity instead of marking you wrong.
  4. Score points. Harder questions pay more. In timer mode, faster correct answers earn a bonus.
  5. Save and share. Finish your run, post your score and challenge a friend.

About us

Logic or Illogic is a brain game for people who are tired of ordinary quizzes. Our questions come from a hand-built bank of 200+ prompts and from an AI that invents new ones on demand, so no two sessions feel the same.

We believe a good question can be a puzzle, a joke or a doorway. Sometimes the best answer is a correct one. Sometimes it's the weirdest one.
