Strategic Advocacy. Personalized Representation. Results That Matter.

At Daniels McLaurin, PLLC, we understand that legal issues are rarely just legal issues; they affect families, businesses, livelihoods, reputations, and futures. As a boutique law firm based in Washington, D.C., we provide sophisticated legal representation with the individualized attention clients deserve.

Our firm is built on the belief that effective advocacy requires more than legal knowledge alone. It requires strategy, responsiveness, attention to detail, and a genuine commitment to understanding each client’s unique goals and challenges. Whether we are negotiating a settlement, protecting your rights in court, or helping you navigate a complex dispute, we approach every matter with diligence, precision, and determination.

Family Law

We provide compassionate and strategic family law representation tailored to each client’s needs. Our services include divorce, child custody and support, parental rights, adoption, agreements, and domestic violence matters, helping families achieve practical solutions, stability, and long-term peace of mind during challenging legal situations.

Business & Contracts Law

We provide strategic legal counsel for businesses, entrepreneurs, and professionals facing contractual disputes and commercial conflicts. Our services include contract litigation, breach of contract claims, fraudulent misrepresentation matters, and arbitration or alternative dispute resolution, helping clients protect their interests and minimize financial risk.

Real Estate Law

We represent property owners, investors, buyers, and developers in residential and commercial real estate matters. Our services include real estate litigation, construction disputes, land sales and leases, purchase options, and zoning or land use matters, protecting clients’ investments and legal interests.

Landlord-Tenant Law

We assist landlords and tenants with residential and commercial leasing disputes and legal matters. Our services include lease drafting and review, evictions, notices to cure, lease enforcement, and HOA or property management disputes, providing practical solutions and effective representation throughout the process.

Personal Injury Law

We help injury victims pursue fair compensation after accidents and negligence-related incidents. Our personal injury services include automobile, bicycle, and pedestrian accidents, dog bites, animal attacks, and negligent security claims, providing compassionate legal support and strong representation throughout the recovery process.

Criminal Defense

We provide skilled and aggressive criminal defense representation for clients facing serious charges in Washington, D.C. and Maryland. Our services include misdemeanor and felony defense, drug offenses, domestic violence charges, weapons violations, violent crimes, and protection or anti-stalking matters.
